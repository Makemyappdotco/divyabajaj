## Life-area rhythm

Every one of the seven life areas follows the same sequence:

1. Short introduction
2. What the birth chart shows
3. What the numbers show
4. Where the two systems agree
5. Where they pull differently, or where one system is silent
6. Timing only where the connected data supports it
7. Practical actions
8. Confidence level and its basis

## Method and safety rules

- KP astrology is the primary map and numerology is an independent second opinion.
- A separate Nadi calculation is not claimed unless a verified Nadi source is connected.
- Convergence is reported only when both systems support it.
- Tension and silence are valid findings and must not be forced into agreement.
- The timing map uses five deterministic Personal Year calculations, covering the report year and the next four calendar years.
- Exact external event dates are not claimed without verified current-residence transit confirmation.
- Past-life content is symbolic, not a factual historical claim.
- Health content does not diagnose and must direct the reader toward qualified medical care.
- Children content does not make fertility claims.
- Gemstones are not recommended in the integrated report.
- Behavioural remedies come first, professional and structural actions second, and optional traditional observances last.

## Length target

The full generated report should be approximately 5,200 to 6,100 words, with a hard QA range of 4,800 to 7,000 words. With the current PDF layout this is intended to produce a focused report of roughly 14 to 18 A4 pages rather than the previous 20 to 28 page target.
