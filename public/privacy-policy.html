1. What this page covers

This page explains what information we collect on this website, how we use it, and the terms under which we provide the free report, the paid Full Blueprint report, and the Private Consultation service (together, the "Services"). It applies to everyone who visits this website or uses the Services.

2. Information we collect

When you request a free report, order the Full Blueprint, or book a consultation, we collect the details you provide, which may include:

  • Your name, phone number (WhatsApp number) and email address

3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

  • Generate and deliver the report or consultation you asked for

  • Let you know about the Full Blueprint report and Private Consultation, if you have not already used those services, as a follow up to your free report or booking

We work with trusted third-party service providers to generate reports, process payments, and deliver messages by WhatsApp and email. These providers only receive the information needed to perform that specific task, and are not permitted to use your information for their own purposes.

By requesting a free report, ordering the Full Blueprint, or booking a Private Consultation, you agree that Divya Bajaj may message you on WhatsApp and by email about:

You can stop the second kind of message, the occasional update about our paid services, at any time. Reply STOP to any WhatsApp message from us, or send us a message saying you would like to stop, and we will honour that immediately and permanently for that request. Messages confirming a report you asked for, or a call you booked and paid for, are not promotional and will still be sent, since they relate directly to a service you have already requested.
